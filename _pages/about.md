I am a PhD candidate in Computer Engineering at [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu), co-advised by [Prof. Yanzhi Wang](https://coe.northeastern.edu/people/wang-yanzhi/) and [Prof. Xuan Zhang](https://coe.northeastern.edu/people/zhang-xuan-silvia/). I earned my M.S. in Computer Engineering from Northeastern University in 2026 and my B.Sc. in Computer Science from the [University of Tehran](https://ut.ac.ir/en) in 2024.

My research spans two directions:
- **Physical AI** — world action models and vision-language-action (VLA) models, with a focus on making them efficient enough for real-time, real-world deployment. I have hands-on experience across different platforms, including humanoids (Unitree G1, AgiBot) and robotic arms (UR3).
- **Privacy-Preserving and Trustworthy Machine Learning** — privacy and robustness of multimodal models and LLMs, approached through the lens of efficiency.

Feel free to reach out via email if you'd like to discuss research or potential collaborations.
