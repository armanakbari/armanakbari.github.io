I am a first-year PhD student in Computer Engineering at [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu), co-advised by [Prof. Yanzhi Wang](https://coe.northeastern.edu/people/wang-yanzhi/) and [Prof. Xuan Zhang](https://coe.northeastern.edu/people/zhang-xuan-silvia/).

My research centers on **Efficient AI**. I develop methods to accelerate pre-training, fine-tuning, and inference of generative models. My work includes model compression, optimization for large language and vision models, and designing fast, robust architectures that reduce computational cost without sacrificing performance. Specifically, my interests focus on LLMs, Multi-modal LLMs, and Diffusion Models.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from the [University of Tehran](https://ut.ac.ir/en).

Feel free to reach out via email if you'd like to discuss research or potential collaborations.
