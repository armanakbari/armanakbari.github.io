Research

My research centers on two main areas:

  • Efficient Deep Learning: I develop methods to accelerate pre-training, fine-tuning, and inference of deep neural networks. My work includes model compression, optimization for large language and vision models, and designing fast, robust architectures that reduce computational cost without sacrificing performance.
  • AI for Science: I apply advanced AI and machine learning techniques to solve scientific problems, enabling new discoveries and insights across disciplines. This includes developing models that can analyze complex scientific data, automate experimental processes, and assist in hypothesis generation.

VOTE: Vision-Language-Action Optimization with Trajectory Ensemble Voting
Juyi Lin, Amir Taherin, Arash Akbari, Arman Akbari, Lei Lu, Guangyu Chen, Taskin Padir, Xiaomeng Yang, Weiwei Chen, Yiqian Li, Xue Lin, David Kaeli, Pu Zhao, Yanzhi Wang
[PDF / Code]

In this work, we propose VOTE, an efficient and general framework for the optimization and acceleration of VLA models. In details, we propose a novel tokenizer-free fine-tuning approach for parallel accurate action prediction, which reduces computational overhead and accelerates inference speed. Experimental results show that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance with 35 faster inference and 145 Hz throughput. All the details and codes will be open-sourced.

A 2D Geometry Based Grasping Pose Generation Algorithm for a Two-Finger Robot Hand
Arash Akbari, Arman Akbari, Mehdi Tale Masouleh
[ICEE 2023] International Conference on Electrical Engineering
[PDF]

In this paper, a geometry-based algorithm is presented which can find grasp poses based on the geometry of the unknown object and propose the ones which may lead to successful grasping. For the grasp contacts computation part, the presented algorithm produces a finite number of key points based on the 2D shape of the object from a specific point of view. Afterward, it will narrow down the candidate points and output a finite number of successful grasp poses based on three grasp quality metrics
